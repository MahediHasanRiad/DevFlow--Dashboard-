# Workspace Development Guidelines

These rules apply across the entire workspace.

## Core Principles
1. **Quality & Maintainability**: Write modular, clean, and readable code.
2. **Error Handling**: Implement graceful error handling with informative messages.
3. **Documentation**: Document complex business logic, public APIs, and non-obvious design choices.
4. **Performance**: Avoid premature optimization, but maintain efficient algorithms and minimize unnecessary re-renders or allocations.
